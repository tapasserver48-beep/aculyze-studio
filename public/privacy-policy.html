1.Information we collect

Aculyze AI Studio is a workspace application for research, sales intelligence and task management. We collect only the information needed to run the product.

Information you provide

  • Account details — workspace name and profile information you enter when setting up the studio.
  • Messages and prompts — the questions you ask Zara (research assistant) and the Sales Agent, and the replies you receive.
  • Inbox items — emails and notes you send to your Aculyze inbox.
  • Task and plan data — tasks, Chief of Staff plans, approvals and notes you create in the workspace.
  • Contact details — email address or phone number you share when you contact support or use the Store contact form.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, features used and timestamps, kept to diagnose problems and improve the product.
  • Device data — browser type, operating system and screen size used to render the interface correctly.
  • Diagnostic logs — server-side error and request logs used to keep the service reliable.
In short: we do not sell your data, we do not use your conversations for advertising, and your chat history stays in your workspace — not in a shared pool.

2.How we use your information

  • Provide the service — answer your prompts, run crawls, generate plans, and display your tasks and inbox.
  • Personalise your workspace — remember theme, compact mode, animation and voice-reply preferences in your browser.
  • Secure the product — detect abuse, protect accounts and keep unauthorised users out of tenant data.
  • Support you — respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues you report.
  • Improve the product — understand which features are used and where the experience breaks down, using aggregated or de-identified signals where possible.

3.AI processing

When you send a prompt, the relevant text is forwarded to the configured AI provider so a reply can be generated.

  • Provider: NVIDIA (NVIDIA API / NIM endpoints) is the AI provider for this deployment. Model requests, including prompt text and conversation context needed for the reply, are processed by NVIDIA.
  • API keys: provider keys are stored server-side only. They are never sent to, or readable from, your browser.
  • Context window: only the conversation context required for the current reply is sent with each request.
  • Web research: if you enable web research, fetched page content may be included in the prompt to produce a sourced answer.
  • No training on your data: we do not submit your workspace content for model training.
Prompts are sent only to the AI provider configured for this workspace. Conversations are stored in your workspace so you can resume them — they are not shared across tenants.

6.Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in these situations:

  • Service providers — the AI and hosting providers above, strictly to operate the product.
  • Legal requirements — when disclosure is required by law, regulation or valid legal process.
  • Protection of rights — to protect the security of the service, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you.

7.Data security

  • API keys and credentials are held server-side and never exposed to the browser.
  • Tenant authorisation checks guard every workspace-scoped endpoint.
  • Website crawling runs behind SSRF protections (blocked private networks, DNS-rebinding checks) so crawler access cannot be turned against internal systems.
  • Public chatbot endpoints require a human approval gate before anything is published.
  • You can wipe local conversations at any time from Settings → Security → Clear local data.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required.

8.Your rights

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your data, subject to legal retention needs.
  • Export — request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is consent-based, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We respond within a reasonable period, and within 30 days where applicable law requires it.

9.Children's privacy

Aculyze AI Studio is a business product and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

10.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the product or the law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the product. Continued use of the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
