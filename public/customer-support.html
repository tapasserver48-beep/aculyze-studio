4.Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Chats with Zara and the Sales Agent stay in your browser's local storage. Inbox messages, tasks, plans and crawled knowledge are stored server-side in your workspace (or PostgreSQL when a database is configured). Workspace data is isolated per tenant — details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which AI provider does Aculyze use?

This deployment uses NVIDIA models exclusively. Prompts are sent to the NVIDIA API for inference, and API keys are kept server-side only — they are never exposed to your browser.

How do I clear my conversations or local data?

Open Settings → Security → Clear local data. This removes locally stored conversations and preferences from your browser. Server-side inbox items can be deleted individually from the Inbox.

Can I crawl my own website into the knowledge base?

Yes. Use the Customer Executive workspace to create a crawl job for your site. Pages are fetched with security checks (private networks are blocked), and extracted content is stored as versioned, tenant-isolated knowledge your chatbot can answer from.

How does the public chatbot approval gate work?

Nothing generated from a crawl goes live automatically. A draft must be reviewed and approved by a human in your workspace before it is published to the public chatbot endpoint.

Do I need an API key to use the studio?

No key is required in your browser. Provider keys are configured server-side by your administrator. If AI replies are unavailable, the provider key may be missing or expired — contact support and we'll check.

How do I change my theme or voice replies?

Everything lives in Settings → Account: light/dark theme, compact mode, animations and voice replies. Changes apply immediately and persist in your browser.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email admin@aculyze.in with the subject "Bug report" or "Feature request". Include what you were doing, what you expected, and a screenshot if you can — it gets to the team faster.
